    Built the first BTC 15m futures scaffold and free-data pipeline.

  • 2026-06-14 Marked drawdown fixed

    Changed the backtest to mark open positions intrabar so drawdown is not understated.

  • 2026-06-14 Public benchmark added

    Ported a Turtle-style Donchian breakout so we can compare against a widely studied system.

  • 2026-06-14 Twelve-month validation

    Mean reversion stayed positive over a full year; Turtle was positive but weaker.

  • 2026-06-14 Regime split check

    The mean-reversion lead stayed positive in both halves of the sample, not just one lucky segment.

  • 2026-06-14 Funding and slippage checks

    BTC perp funding was measured from the Binance funding feed and slippage was stressed at higher assumptions.

  • 2026-06-14 Rolling window check

    The lead strategy was tested across sequential 3-month windows to see whether the edge degrades by regime.

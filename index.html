Research map

Candidate families considered

  • Simple trend pullback continuation

    Trend and momentum are among the better documented BTC effects, and pullbacks avoid chasing the candle high.

    Exploit: Continuation after a higher-timeframe trend resumes from a shallow intraday pullback.

    Fragile because: Chop creates repeated fake pullbacks, especially when the trend filter lags.

    Execution: Limit fills matter, but the edge must survive missed fills and maker/taker costs.

  • Looser mean reversion

    The current 15m lead is a mean-reversion strategy, so loosening it is the simplest way to seek more trades.

    Exploit: Short-term overshoot back toward a Bollinger midline inside a filtered regime.

    Fragile because: Small reversions get eaten by spread and latency; looser filters admit trend-day losers.

    Execution: Needs maker-style fills and hard spread/volatility gates.

  • Session-filtered BTC behavior

    Recent public research documents strong Bitcoin intraday and intraweek seasonal structure.

    Exploit: Activity around Asia or US session windows.

    Fragile because: Seasonality is thin and can vanish after spread and slippage.

    Execution: Requires clock-accurate scheduling and strict skip rules around wide spread.

  • Volatility breakout / squeeze expansion

    BTC volatility clusters and breakouts are simple to deploy.

    Exploit: Range compression followed by directional expansion.

    Fragile because: Breakout entries are usually taker-like and highly slippage-sensitive.

    Execution: Needs conservative spread, latency, and false-breakout controls.

  • Funding-aware directional fade

    Funding is structural to perpetual futures and can proxy crowding.

    Exploit: Crowded long or short positioning around funding windows.

    Fragile because: Funding is not a timing signal by itself, and directional price movement dominates small funding edge.

    Execution: Must account for real funding, maker/taker fees, and holding-period risk.

  • Passivbot / market-making style contrarian logic

    Passivbot is documented and deployable, but a faithful version needs order handling beyond simple OHLCV.

    Exploit: Passive noise harvesting around fair value.

    Fragile because: Inventory risk and adverse selection dominate if fills are modeled too generously.

    Execution: Requires order book or at least realistic queue/fill modeling.
